Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. You can work on Intermediate topics while still solidifying Junior fundamentals.

Intermediate to Senior is a shift in judgment, not tools. Knowing when not to use a technique matters as much as knowing how.
