Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Threads appear at Junior as a first taste, return at Intermediate with synchronization, and come back at Senior as atomics and lock-free design. Memory works the same way: layout, then virtual memory, then hardware effects. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about diagnosis and judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how a page fault or a futex works and being able to find it in a misbehaving production system, then explain it, are different skills, and the last stage is built around that gap.
