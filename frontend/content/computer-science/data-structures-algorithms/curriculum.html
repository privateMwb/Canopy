Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Dynamic programming shows up as early as Intermediate and gets revisited at Senior with real depth — revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected, not a sign you skipped something.

Senior is about judgment and communication as much as technique. Knowing an algorithm and being able to explain why you'd use it are different skills, and the last stage is built around that gap.
