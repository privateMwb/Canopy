Streams & Consumer Groups

A stream is an append-only log with ids, and consumer groups let several workers share it while Redis tracks what each has acknowledged.

§1Appending and Reading

XADD orders * customer 42 total 19.99     # * = auto id like 1715680000000-0
XLEN orders
XRANGE orders - + COUNT 10                # oldest to newest
XREAD COUNT 10 BLOCK 5000 STREAMS orders $   # wait for new entries

Entries are immutable and stay in the stream until you trim it. XADD orders MAXLEN ~ 100000 * ... keeps it bounded, and the ~ allows efficient approximate trimming.

§2Consumer Groups

A group divides entries among its consumers, and each entry goes to one consumer in the group.

XGROUP CREATE orders billing $ MKSTREAM
XREADGROUP GROUP billing worker-1 COUNT 5 BLOCK 5000 STREAMS orders >
XACK orders billing 1715680000000-0       # done with this entry
XPENDING orders billing                   # delivered but not yet acknowledged

Until XACK, an entry sits in the group's pending list. If a worker dies, another can take over its entries with XAUTOCLAIM after an idle time.

The key insight Streams give you what Pub/Sub does not: persistence, replay, acknowledgement, and recovery from a crashed consumer.

§3Common Mistakes

MistakeWhat HappensFix
Never calling XACKThe pending list grows and entries are redeliveredAcknowledge after successful processing
Never trimming the streamMemory grows without boundUse MAXLEN ~ or XTRIM by size or id
Ignoring pending entries from dead workersMessages stall foreverRun XAUTOCLAIM on idle entries
Practice
  1. Create a stream and a group with two consumers, process ten entries, and leave three unacknowledged. Then claim them from a third consumer.
  2. Add trimming and verify the stream length stays near the limit.
Key takeaways
  • Streams are append-only logs with ids and optional trimming.
  • Consumer groups share entries between workers and track acknowledgements.
  • Unacknowledged entries can be claimed by another consumer.
