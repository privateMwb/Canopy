Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Caching appears at Junior as cache-aside, returns at Intermediate as stampedes and invalidation, and comes back at Senior as consistency and the decision about what Redis should hold at all. Persistence follows the same arc: snapshots and AOF, then rewrite and replication, then failure modes. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how the event loop or cluster gossip works is one skill; deciding whether Redis should be a cache, a queue, or a source of truth, and writing that down for a skeptical reviewer, is another, and the last stage is built around that gap.
