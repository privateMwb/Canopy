Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Ownership appears at Junior as RAII and smart pointers, returns at Intermediate as move semantics, and comes back at Senior as lifetime, provenance, and the memory model. Templates follow the same arc: writing them, then constraining them with concepts, then designing with them. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Senior is about what the machine and the optimizer do with your code. Knowing the rules of undefined behavior and being able to find the one violation in a crash you can't reproduce are different skills, and the last stages are built around that gap.
