Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Async code appears at Junior as promises and fetch, returns at Intermediate as the event loop and cancellation, and comes back at Senior as streams, workers, and runtime internals. Types work the same way: coercion first, then TypeScript, then engine-level behavior. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Senior is about the engine, the attack surface, and the architecture as much as the syntax. Knowing why an object's shape affects speed and being able to defend a rendering strategy to a team are different skills, and the last two stages are built around that gap.
