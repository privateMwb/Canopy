Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Testing arrives at Junior as a checker and a keyboard, returns at Intermediate as screen readers and repeatable test plans, and comes back at Senior as programs, governance, and conformance reporting. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how the accessibility tree exposes a widget is one skill; deciding which barrier to fix first, and persuading a leadership team to fund it, is another, and the last stage is built around that gap.
