Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Semantics arrive at Junior as the right tag for the job, return at Intermediate as the accessibility tree and native interactivity, and come back at Senior as parser behavior and markup contracts for whole teams. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how the parser recovers from bad markup is one skill; deciding when to use a native element, when to build a custom one, and defending that choice to a team is another, and the last stage is built around that gap.
