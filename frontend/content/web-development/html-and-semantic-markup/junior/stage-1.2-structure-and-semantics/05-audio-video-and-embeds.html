Audio, Video & Embeds

audio and video play media without plugins; track adds captions; and iframe embeds other pages safely when you set sensible defaults.

§1Video and Audio

Both elements accept multiple <source> children so the browser can pick a format it supports. Always include controls, since without it there is no visible way to play or pause.

<video controls width="640" poster="preview.jpg" preload="metadata">
  <source src="demo.webm" type="video/webm">
  <source src="demo.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  <track kind="captions" src="demo.en.vtt" srclang="en" label="English" default>
  <p>Your browser can't play this video. <a href="demo.mp4">Download it</a>.</p>
</video>

<audio controls src="episode-12.mp3">
  <a href="episode-12.mp3">Download the episode</a>
</audio>

Autoplaying media with sound is blocked by most browsers and unwelcome to users. If you must autoplay, use muted and avoid it for anything with important audio.

§2Captions and Transcripts

<track kind="captions"> links a WebVTT file with timed text so Deaf and hard-of-hearing users, and anyone watching without sound, can follow along. Captions are a requirement for most public video, and a transcript also helps for audio-only content.

WEBVTT

00:00:01.000 --> 00:00:04.000
Welcome to the Canopy tour.

00:00:04.500 --> 00:00:08.000
Today we will look at forest layers.

§3Embeds with iframe

<iframe> embeds another page, such as a map or third-party video. Give it a title so screen readers can name it, and treat it as untrusted code running on your page.

<iframe
  src="https://www.example.com/embed/map"
  title="Map of our office location"
  width="600" height="400"
  loading="lazy"
  referrerpolicy="no-referrer"
  sandbox="allow-scripts allow-same-origin"></iframe>

loading="lazy" defers off-screen frames, and sandbox restricts what the embedded content can do. Only loosen the sandbox as far as the embed requires.

Provide a fallback Put a download link inside video and audio. Browsers that support the element ignore it, and everyone else still gets the media.

§4Common Mistakes

MistakeWhat HappensFix
Using controls-less videoKeyboard and screen reader users can't operate itInclude controls or build fully accessible custom controls
Embedding video with no captionsInaccessible to users who can't hear itAdd a track with captions and consider a transcript
Leaving iframe without a titleAnnounced as an unnamed frameGive a short, descriptive title
Embedding content from sources you do not trustThat page can run scripts in your visitors' browsersUse sandbox, and only embed what you trust
Practice
  1. Embed a short video with a poster, two sources, and an English caption track.
  2. Embed a map with an iframe that has a title, lazy loading, and a sandbox.
Key takeaways
  • Always add controls, fallback links, and captions for media.
  • Give every iframe a title, lazy loading where fitting, and a sandbox.
  • Offer multiple source formats and don't autoplay audio.
