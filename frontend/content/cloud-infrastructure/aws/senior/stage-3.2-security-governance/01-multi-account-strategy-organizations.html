Multi-Account Strategy & Organizations

An AWS account is the strongest isolation boundary available, so serious environments use many accounts governed together through AWS Organizations.

§1Why Many Accounts

  • Blast radius: a compromise or mistake stays inside one account.
  • Isolation: production, development, and security tooling separated by a hard boundary.
  • Billing and quotas: clear cost attribution and independent service limits.
  • Delegation: teams get autonomy inside guardrails.
  • Compliance: regulated workloads get their own scoped environment.

§2Organizations, OUs, and SCPs

AWS Organizations groups accounts into organizational units (OUs) and applies service control policies (SCPs): guardrails that set the maximum permissions for every principal in an account, including its root user. SCPs never grant access; they limit it.

{
  "Effect": "Deny",
  "Action": "*",
  "Resource": "*",
  "Condition": {"StringNotEquals": {"aws:RequestedRegion": ["eu-west-1", "eu-central-1"]}}
}

Use SCP deny statements for things that should never happen, such as disabling CloudTrail, leaving the organization, or using unapproved regions (with exceptions for global services).

§3Landing Zone Structure

Root
├── Security OU      → log-archive, security-tooling accounts
├── Infrastructure OU → network, shared-services accounts
├── Workloads OU
│   ├── Prod OU      → one account per workload
│   └── NonProd OU   → dev/test accounts
└── Sandbox OU       → experimental, budget-capped, auto-cleaned

AWS Control Tower builds a landing zone with these foundations, account vending, and preventive and detective controls. The management account should hold almost nothing: no workloads, minimal users.

The management account is the crown jewel SCPs do not apply to the management account, and it controls the whole organization. Keep it empty of workloads and locked down tightly.

§4Common Mistakes

MistakeWhat HappensFix
Running workloads in the management accountUnrestricted by SCPs and a high-value targetKeep it for governance only
SCPs with no testingLocking teams out of required actionsTest on a sandbox OU before wide rollout
One account for all environmentsLarge blast radius and tangled IAMSeparate by environment and sensitivity
Practice
  1. Draw an OU structure for a company with prod, dev, security tooling, and a sandbox.
  2. Write an SCP that denies disabling CloudTrail and test it in a sandbox account.
Key takeaways
  • Accounts are the strongest isolation boundary; use many of them.
  • SCPs set maximum permissions across accounts and OUs but never grant access.
  • Keep the management account minimal and use Control Tower for baselines.
