Serverless vs. Containers vs. Instances

No compute model is best; the right one depends on traffic shape, team skills, latency needs, and how much operations work you want.

§1The Trade-Offs

LambdaContainers (Fargate/ECS/EKS)EC2 instances
Ops effortLowestMediumHighest
ScalingPer request, to zeroPer task or pod; minutesASG; minutes
Max run time15 minutesUnlimitedUnlimited
Cost shapePay per use; costly at steady high loadPay per provisioned CPU/memoryCheapest at steady high utilization, with commitments
ControlLimitedHighTotal (OS, kernel, GPUs)
Cold startPossibleTask start timeInstance start time

§2When Each Wins

  • Lambda: spiky or low-volume traffic, event glue, short tasks, small teams who want minimal operations.
  • Containers: long-running services, existing container workflows, portability, and moderate control.
  • EC2: steady heavy load where utilization is high, special hardware, licensing, or kernel-level needs.

A common pattern: Lambda for events and glue, containers for the core services, and EC2 only for what truly needs it. Mixed fleets are normal.

§3Deciding Honestly

Questions:
1. Is traffic spiky or steady? (spiky favors serverless; steady favors provisioned)
2. Longest request / job duration?
3. Latency target and tolerance for cold starts?
4. Team's operational capacity and existing skills?
5. Cost at 10x current volume?
6. Exit cost: how hard is it to move off?

Do the arithmetic at your expected volume. A function that costs pennies at 1 million requests may cost more than a small container fleet at a billion.

Do the math at your scale The cheapest option at low volume is rarely the cheapest at high volume. Model both ends and note where the lines cross.

§4Common Mistakes

Picking Kubernetes with no team to run itOperational burdenMatch platform to team capacity
Practice
  1. Estimate monthly cost of one API at 1M and 1B requests per month on Lambda versus Fargate.
  2. Write down which model you would pick for three different workloads and why.
Key takeaways
  • Each compute model trades operational effort, cost shape, control, and limits.
  • Mixed use is normal: serverless for events, containers for services, EC2 for special needs.
  • Model costs at both current and future volume.
