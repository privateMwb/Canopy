Service Quotas, Limits & Capacity Planning

Every AWS account has quotas, and the ones you do not know about tend to appear at the worst possible moment.

§1Soft Limits and Hard Limits

Service Quotas lists limits per service and region: vCPU counts, VPCs, Elastic IPs, Lambda concurrency, API rates, and more. Many are adjustable with a request; some are fixed. New accounts often start with lower quotas.

aws service-quotas list-service-quotas --service-code ec2 \
  --query 'Quotas[?Adjustable].[QuotaName,Value]'
aws service-quotas request-service-quota-increase \
  --service-code lambda --quota-code L-B99A9384 --desired-value 3000

§2API Throttling

Besides resource quotas there are API rate limits. Calls beyond them return throttling errors such as ThrottlingException or RequestLimitExceeded. SDKs retry with backoff, but heavy automation and large fleets can still hit limits. Spread calls, cache describe results, and request increases when justified.

§3Planning Capacity

  • List the quotas your design depends on and their current values, per region and account.
  • Compare with expected peak plus growth, including failover load: DR regions need quota headroom too.
  • Monitor usage with CloudWatch metrics for quotas and alarm at a percentage of the limit.
  • Request increases early; some take days and need justification.
  • For scarce capacity such as certain instance types, consider On-Demand Capacity Reservations and flexible instance diversification.
  • Multi-account strategy raises effective limits and reduces blast radius.
Alarm: vCPU usage > 80% of quota → ticket to request increase (lead time 3 days)
Check the DR region's quotas A failover to a region where your quotas are still the new-account defaults will fail exactly when you need it. Align quotas across regions in advance.

§4Common Mistakes

MistakeWhat HappensFix
Discovering quotas during a launchFailed scale-out or deploymentReview quotas as part of readiness
Forgetting DR region quotasFailover can't launch capacityRaise quotas in recovery regions
No alarms on quota usageHitting limits without warningAlarm on utilization percentages
Practice
  1. List quotas that would limit your last project and compare with its peak load.
  2. Create an alarm on a quota usage metric.
Key takeaways
  • Quotas exist per service, region, and account; some are adjustable.
  • Plan for growth and failover, and alarm before reaching limits.
  • Request increases early and keep DR regions aligned.
