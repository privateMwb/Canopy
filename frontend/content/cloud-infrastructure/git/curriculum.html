Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Undoing mistakes appears at Junior as restore and amend, returns at Intermediate as reset, revert, and the reflog, and comes back at Senior as repository recovery and rewriting history after a leaked secret. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing that a commit is a tree and a pointer is one skill; choosing trunk-based development or release branches for a team of twelve, and writing the policy so people actually follow it, is another, and the last stage is built around that gap.
