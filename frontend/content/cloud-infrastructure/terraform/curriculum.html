Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. State appears at Junior as the file that maps code to resources, returns at Intermediate as remote backends and locking, and comes back at Senior as surgery, recovery, and drift. Safety follows the same arc: reading plans, then testing and policy, then blast radius. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how Terraform builds its graph is one skill; deciding how to split state, who owns which module, and what should deliberately stay out of code, is another, and the last stage is built around that gap.
