Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Modeling appears at Junior as embedding versus referencing, returns at Intermediate as a catalog of patterns, and comes back at Senior as shard keys and trade-offs at scale. Consistency follows the same arc: single-document atomicity, then write and read concerns, then replication and failure behavior. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how WiredTiger journals or how a shard key distributes load is one skill; deciding whether MongoDB should hold a workload at all, and writing that down for a skeptical reviewer, is another, and the last stage is built around that gap.
