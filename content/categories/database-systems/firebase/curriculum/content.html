Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Security rules appear at Junior as ownership checks, return at Intermediate as validation and roles, and come back at Senior as threat modeling and abuse prevention. Cost follows the same arc: reads and writes, then listener behavior, then forecasting and architecture. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how Firestore indexes or hotspots behave is one skill; deciding whether Firebase should be the backend at all, planning the exit, and writing that down for a skeptical reviewer, is another, and the last stage is built around that gap.
