Sentinel & Automatic Failover

Sentinel watches a primary and its replicas, promotes a replica when the primary fails, and tells clients where the new primary is.

§1What Sentinel Does

You run three or more Sentinel processes. They monitor the primary, agree it is down by quorum, elect one Sentinel to lead, promote the best replica, and update the other replicas to follow it.

sentinel monitor mymaster 10.0.0.5 6379 2        # quorum of 2
sentinel down-after-milliseconds mymaster 5000
sentinel failover-timeout mymaster 60000
SENTINEL get-master-addr-by-name mymaster
SENTINEL replicas mymaster

§2Why Three

With two Sentinels you cannot get a majority if one fails. Place Sentinels on separate machines or zones so one failure does not remove a majority.

§3Client Behavior

Clients must be Sentinel-aware: they ask Sentinel for the current primary and reconnect when it changes. A client with a hard-coded address will keep trying the dead node.

const redis = createClient({ sentinels: [{ host: "s1" }, { host: "s2" }, { host: "s3" }], name: "mymaster" });

§4What You Can Lose

Failover takes seconds, during which writes fail. Writes that had not reached the promoted replica are lost. To reduce loss, set min-replicas-to-write 1 so the primary stops accepting writes when it has no replicas, which limits how far it can drift if it is partitioned away from the rest.

Gotcha A partitioned old primary may keep accepting writes from some clients while a new primary is elected, creating divergent data that is discarded on rejoin.

§5Common Mistakes

MistakeWhat HappensFix
Two Sentinels onlyNo majority after one failureRun at least three on separate hosts
Hard-coded primary address in clientsClients keep writing to the dead nodeUse Sentinel-aware clients
Ignoring min-replicas-to-writeA partitioned primary accepts writes that will be lostRequire at least one replica
Practice
  1. Build one primary, two replicas, and three Sentinels, then stop the primary and time the failover.
  2. Verify what your client does during failover and how long writes fail.
Key takeaways
  • Sentinel provides monitoring, quorum-based failover, and discovery.
  • Run at least three Sentinels on separate failure domains.
  • Clients must follow Sentinel, and failover can still lose recent writes.
