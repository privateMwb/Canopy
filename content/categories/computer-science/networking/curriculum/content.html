Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. TCP appears at Junior as handshakes and sockets, returns at Intermediate as congestion control and timers, and comes back at Senior as kernel buffers and packet-level debugging. HTTP follows the same arc. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about diagnosis and judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how a protocol works and being able to find the problem in a misbehaving production system, then explain it, are different skills, and the last stage is built around that gap.
