Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Caches appear at Junior as locality and hit rates, return at Intermediate as associativity, write policies, and simulators, and come back at Senior as coherence, side channels, and performance engineering. The pipeline and memory system follow the same arc. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about diagnosis and judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how a branch predictor or a cache works and being able to find its effect in a slow program, measure it, and explain the tradeoff, are different skills, and the last stage is built around that gap.
