Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Containers appear at Intermediate as a tool, return at Senior as scheduling and resource limits, and connect back to namespaces and cgroups in the Operating Systems track. Security and observability follow the same pattern: basics early, rigor later. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about operating and judgment as much as tooling. Writing a pipeline and keeping a platform reliable, secure, and understood by ten teams are different skills, and the last stages are built around that gap.
