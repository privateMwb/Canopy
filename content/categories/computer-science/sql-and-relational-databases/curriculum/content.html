Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Transactions appear at Junior as BEGIN and COMMIT, return at Intermediate as isolation levels and locking, and come back at Senior as write-ahead logs and distributed commit. Indexes follow the same arc. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how a storage engine works and being able to find the problem in a misbehaving production database, then explain it, are different skills, and the last stage is built around that gap.
