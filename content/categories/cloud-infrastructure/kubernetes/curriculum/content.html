Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Configuration appears at Junior as ConfigMaps and probes, returns at Intermediate as Helm and Kustomize overlays, and comes back at Senior as admission policy and secret management. Debugging follows the same arc: events and logs, then metrics and traces, then incident response. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how the control plane reconciles state is one skill; deciding whether a ten-person company should run Kubernetes at all, and arguing against your own recommendation, is another, and the last stage is built around that gap.
