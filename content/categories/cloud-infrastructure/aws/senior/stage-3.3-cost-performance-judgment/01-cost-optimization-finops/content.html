Cost Optimization & FinOps

FinOps treats cloud cost as an engineering metric owned by the teams that cause it, with levers ranging from deleting waste to committing to usage.

§1Where Savings Come From

LeverIdeaRisk
Eliminate wasteDelete idle resources, old snapshots, unattached volumesLow
RightsizeMatch instance size to measured use (Compute Optimizer helps)Low to medium
Use the right architectureGraviton, serverless, managed services, storage tiersMedium; needs change
CommitSavings Plans or Reserved Instances for steady baseline usageOver-commitment wastes money
SpotUp to deep discounts for interruptible workloadsInterruptions

§2Commitments in Practice

Savings Plans trade a one- or three-year hourly spend commitment for lower rates. Compute Savings Plans apply flexibly across instance families, regions, Fargate, and Lambda; EC2 Instance Savings Plans give deeper discounts for a specific family and region. Commit only to the steady floor of usage you are confident will persist, and ladder commitments over time rather than buying everything at once.

§3Making Cost a Shared Responsibility

  • Allocation: consistent tags and accounts so every dollar has an owner.
  • Visibility: Cost Explorer, Cost and Usage Reports, and per-team dashboards.
  • Budgets and anomaly detection: alerts that reach the team that can act.
  • Unit economics: cost per customer, request, or order, which shows efficiency, not just spend.
  • Cadence: a regular review with engineering and finance together.
Total bill up 20%  ...but cost per order down 15%  → growth, not waste
Total bill flat     ...but cost per order up 30%    → efficiency problem
Unit cost beats total cost A rising bill can be healthy growth and a flat one can hide decay. Divide cost by something the business cares about to tell which it is.

§4Common Mistakes

MistakeWhat HappensFix
Buying large commitments before rightsizingLocking in wasteRightsize first, then commit to the steady floor
No tag or account ownershipNobody feels responsibleAllocate cost to teams automatically
Centralizing cost work in finance onlyEngineers never see the effect of choicesGive teams their own cost data and goals
Practice
  1. List the five largest items on a bill and one lever you would apply to each.
  2. Calculate cost per request for a service and track it for a month.
Key takeaways
  • Savings come from waste removal, rightsizing, architecture, commitments, and Spot.
  • Commit only to a stable baseline and ladder purchases.
  • Track unit costs and give teams ownership of their spend.
