Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Measurement arrives at Junior as a Lighthouse report, returns at Intermediate as traces, waterfalls, and real-user data, and comes back at Senior as budgets, regression pipelines, and performance culture. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how the browser prioritizes requests is one skill; deciding what to fix first when everything is slow, and proving the business value to leadership, is another, and the last stage is built around that gap.
