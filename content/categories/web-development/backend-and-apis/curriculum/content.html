From your first endpoint to APIs other teams can rely on

Three levels, twelve stages — HTTP, servers, data, and authentication, built toward backend services that are reliable, secure, and easy for others to integrate with. Examples use Node.js, but the concepts apply to any backend stack.

Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Data arrives at Junior as tables and queries, returns at Intermediate as indexes, transactions, and caching, and comes back at Senior as replication, sharding, and consistency trade-offs. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how a queue or a circuit breaker works is one skill; deciding where a service boundary belongs, and getting a team to agree, is another, and the last stage is built around that gap.
