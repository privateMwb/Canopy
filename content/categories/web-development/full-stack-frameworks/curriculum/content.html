Three levels, twelve stages — routing, rendering, data, and deployment in a modern framework such as Next.js, built toward full-stack apps that are secure, fast, and maintainable. The concepts carry over to Remix, Nuxt, and SvelteKit.

Checkpoints are deliberately unscaffolded. If you can finish one without hints, the stage actually landed, not just the following-along.

Levels aren't strict walls. Rendering arrives at Junior as server vs. client components, returns at Intermediate as caching, streaming, and rendering modes, and comes back at Senior as how the framework's layers decide what is fresh. Revisiting a topic at a harder level is expected.

Senior is about judgment as much as mechanism. Knowing how a framework's caches and runtimes behave is one skill; deciding whether to adopt it, self-host it, or skip it for something simpler, and writing that down, is another, and the last stage is built around that gap.
